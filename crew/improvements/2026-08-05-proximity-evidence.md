# Distinguish destination and proximity evidence

## Evidence

Two isolated runs treated the distant destination ring as proof that the avatar
was in interaction range. The dedicated bottom-center `E` prompt had not
appeared, and early interaction attempts were therefore misclassified.

## Knowledge change

The reviewed heuristic now requires both visible movement and the dedicated
interaction prompt before an agent diagnoses an in-range interaction failure.
This does not reveal the journey route or hidden state.

`output/playwright/hardware-visible-acceptance/02-guide-range.png` and
`07-guide-action.png` show the visible prompt followed by Guide panel opening
