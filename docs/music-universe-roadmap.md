# Music Universe Roadmap

## Vision

Turn music into worlds people can enter.

The project evolves from a generic spatial UI toolkit into an application runtime for AI-generated music worlds.

## Product Concept

A user uploads a track. AI analyzes the music, gathers available context, creates a world bible and interactive narrative, then builds a small explorable planet where the listener becomes the main character.

## Core Pipeline

```
Audio
  -> Music analysis
  -> World bible
  -> Narrative timeline
  -> Spatial application schema
  -> 3D assets/world generation
  -> Interactive experience
  -> Community publishing
```

## MVP

### Experience

- 3-5 minute interactive music journey
- Small planet metaphor
- Time-synchronized environment changes
- Limited interaction points
- Shareable public experience pages

### Generation

1. Analyze audio structure and emotion
2. Generate creative interpretation
3. Select experience template
4. Generate scene assets
5. Compile runtime interactions

## Experience Templates

- Journey: move through an emotional arc
- Memory: discover fragments of a story
- Encounter: meet characters/entities
- Transformation: world changes with music
- Open Planet: free exploration

## Long Term Platform

A public music world database where creators publish, remix, and explore experiences.

The competitive advantage is not the 3D generator. It is the semantic layer connecting music, narrative, interaction, and spatial applications.

## Cost Strategy

Avoid generating everything from zero.

Use:

- reusable world templates
- asset libraries
- generated landmarks
- runtime effects
- community asset reuse

## Risks

- Music copyright
- Generation consistency
- Content moderation
- Cost control

## Initial Users

Independent musicians and creators who need immersive music experiences.
